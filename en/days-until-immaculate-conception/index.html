How many days until 8 December?

The big number at the top of the page is the answer: the whole days left until 8 December, today excluded. It's a fixed date, so only the weekday changes — it's written under the count together with the full date. The line with hours, minutes and seconds updates on its own.

Is it a long weekend?

It depends on the weekday: when 8 December falls on a Tuesday or Thursday, one day off makes a four-day break; on a Monday or Friday the weekend is long by itself; on a Saturday or Sunday the holiday is lost (Italy doesn't move it). In 2026, 8 December is a Tuesday.

What happens on 8 December

  • Tree and nativity scene: in Italy they traditionally go up on this day and come down on Epiphany, 6 January.
  • Markets and lights: many cities switch on their Christmas lights on this weekend.
  • Presents: from here to Christmas there are 17 days, and fewer than fifteen working days for deliveries. Christmas Eve is the 24th. For any other date there is the free countdown.

Frequently asked questions

How many days until 8 December?

The whole days between today and the Immaculate Conception: you'll find them in the big number at the top of the page. Once 8 December has passed, the count moves to next year by itself.

Is 8 December a public holiday?

Yes, in Italy the Immaculate Conception is a national holiday: schools, offices and most shops are closed.

What weekday is 8 December this year?

Read it under the big number, together with the full date. In 2026 it's a Tuesday, in 2027 a Wednesday.

When does the Christmas tree go up in Italy?

Traditionally on 8 December, and it comes down on 6 January. Many families start earlier, from 1 December.
