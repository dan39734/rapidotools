When school ends

The last day of lessons is set by each region, state or school district: in Italy between 6 and 10 June, in the UK around 20 July, in the US from late May to mid-June. Enter the date from your school calendar and the countdown does the rest.

Calendar days and school days

The big number is calendar days; the "Working days (Mon–Fri)" box corresponds to school days, without subtracting public holidays and school closures: subtract those by hand.

Ideas for the last weeks

  • Tests and exams: plan revision on the days left.
  • End-of-year trip and class parties: copy the sentence with the count and post it in the class chat.
  • Summer camps: registrations open in spring: the last day of school tells you from when you need them.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I have to enter the date?

Because every region, state or district sets its own, and it changes every year. 8 June is only a placeholder: replace it with the date on your school calendar.

How many school days are really left?

Look at "Working days (Mon–Fri)" and subtract public holidays and your school's closures.

Does the count include the last day?

No: it counts the whole days left. The evening before it shows "1 day".
